Rotisserie Chicken

This rotisserie chicken recipe is prepared on an outdoor grill using a rotisserie attachment and simple seasonings. Occasional basting with a butter and paprika mixture keeps the skin crispy while locking in moisture.

It makes a great main dish served alongside French fries and coleslaw, or sliced for salads, casseroles, and meal prep throughout the week.

Ingredients

  • 1 (3 pound) whole chicken
  • 1 pinch salt (for cavity)
  • ¼ cup butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon ground paprika
  • ¼ tablespoon ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Prep grill: Preheat an outdoor grill to high heat and lightly oil the grate.
  2. Truss and secure chicken: Season the chicken cavity with a pinch of salt. Tie the legs together with kitchen string and secure the wings against the body. Mount the chicken onto the rotisserie spit attachment.
  3. Initial sear: Place the rotisserie spit onto the preheated grill and cook on high heat for 10 minutes.
  4. Make baste and lower heat: Combine the melted butter, 1 tablespoon salt, paprika, and black pepper in a small bowl. Reduce the grill heat to medium, baste the chicken with the butter mixture, and close the grill lid.
  5. Grill and baste: Grill for 1 to 1½ hours, basting occasionally with the butter mixture, until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh (without touching the bone) reads 180°F (83°C).
  6. Rest and carve: Remove the chicken from the rotisserie skewer and let it rest for 10 to 15 minutes before carving.
