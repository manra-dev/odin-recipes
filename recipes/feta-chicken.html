Feta Chicken

Description

Feta Chicken features tender chicken cutlets wrapped around savory tomato-basil flavored feta cheese. Simple yet sensational, this dish delivers a bold, tangy flavor inside a golden breadcrumb crust.

It requires just a few basic ingredients and minimal prep time, making it an ideal choice for a quick weeknight dinner that still feels like a gourmet meal.

Ingredients

  • 6 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 6 ounces tomato-basil flavored feta cheese, crumbled
  • ¼ cup Italian-style dry bread crumbs, divided

Steps

  1. Prep oven and baking dish: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Pound chicken: Place the chicken breasts between two sheets of waxed paper. Gently pound with the flat side of a meat mallet or rolling pin until each breast is about 1/4 inch thick, then remove the waxed paper.
  3. Stuff and fold: Place 1 ounce of crumbled tomato-basil feta cheese in the center of each chicken breast, then fold the chicken cutlet in half to enclose the cheese.
  4. Prep dish with bread crumbs: Spread 2 tablespoons of the Italian-style bread crumbs evenly across the bottom of the prepared baking dish.
  5. Assemble: Arrange the folded chicken breasts in the dish on top of the bread crumbs, then sprinkle the remaining 2 tablespoons of bread crumbs evenly over top.
  6. Bake: Bake in the preheated oven for 25 to 30 minutes, or until the chicken is no longer pink and the juices run clear. Let rest briefly before serving.
