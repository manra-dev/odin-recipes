Chicken Parmesan

Description

Chicken Parmesan is one of the most popular non-pasta dishes in Italian-American cuisine, but traditional restaurant preparations often drown cutlets under heavy sauce, leaving the breading soggy. This recipe keeps the breading crisp by adding just a small amount of tomato sauce on top of each cutlet rather than underneath it.

By mixing finely grated Parmesan directly into panko bread crumbs and baking at 450°F (230°C), the chicken achieves a crunchy crust topped with melted mozzarella, tangy provolone, and fresh basil without losing its crisp texture.

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup panko bread crumbs (or more as needed)
  • ¾ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour (or more if needed)
  • ½ cup olive oil for frying (or as needed)
  • ½ cup prepared tomato sauce
  • ¼ cup fresh mozzarella, cut into small cubes
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh basil
  • ½ cup freshly grated provolone cheese
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepare oven and workstation: Gather all ingredients and preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Pound chicken: Place chicken breasts between two sheets of heavy plastic wrap and pound with the flat side of a meat mallet to a uniform thickness of 1/2 inch.
  3. Season and flour: Season both sides of the cutlets generously with salt and pepper, then sift flour evenly over both sides to create a thin coating.
  4. Bread and rest: Beat the eggs in a shallow bowl. In a separate bowl, mix the panko bread crumbs with 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan cheese. Dip each flour-coated chicken breast into the egg, then press into the bread crumb mixture. Let the breaded chicken sit at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes so the coating adheres properly.
  5. Pan-fry: Heat 1/2 inch of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Fry the chicken in batches for about 2 minutes per side until golden brown (it will finish cooking in the oven).
  6. Assemble: Transfer the fried chicken to a baking dish. Top each cutlet with 2 tablespoons of tomato sauce, followed by equal portions of mozzarella, chopped basil, provolone, remaining Parmesan, and a drizzle of 1/2 teaspoon olive oil.
  7. Bake: Bake for 15 to 20 minutes until the cheese is browned and bubbly, and an instant-read thermometer reads 165°F (74°C) at the thickest part of the chicken. Rest briefly before serving.
